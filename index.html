Independent student project · IADT Business Management

Make course work easier to find, practise and remember.

Cram Deck brings class lessons, short practice, a saved planner, assignment guidance, private notes and placement tools into one study space.

See what’s insideAsk about beta access

Cram Deck is an independent student project, not an official IADT app. Always check Blackboard and the Hub for current assignment briefs and timetable changes.

Built for studying one step at a time

One home for the work around your classes.

The aim is to make it easier to see what you’re looking at, choose one next step and learn from a mistake without digging through long lists.

Learn in small pieces

Lessons are grouped by class and topic, with one clear idea at a time.

Practise with saved guidance

Choose a subject and topic, try a short round, then compare your answer with the saved explanation.

Plan coursework

Use assignment steps to organise your own work. The guides explain structure; they don’t hand over finished assignments.

Keep your own notes

Write notes, attach photos and captions, and keep a separate Placement Journal.

Build Excel confidence

Practise formulas and business calculations through short skill activities.

See your saved timetable

Keep class times and rooms together. College timetable changes should still be checked on the official systems.

Private by default

Your notes aren’t posted to classmates.

Study progress, notes, photos and placement journal entries stay under your account. Cram Deck doesn’t publish student content into shared class materials. If you choose to share a note, you pick the destination yourself.
