Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Cram Deck is an independent student project operated by Fred Bademosi for IADT Business Management students. It is not an official IADT service and is not operated or endorsed by IADT.

What information the app uses

  • Account: your sign-in email and Appwrite account identifier.
  • Study activity: lesson and topic progress, practice results, saved settings and coursework checklist progress.
  • Your content: notes, note revisions, photo attachments and captions, and Placement Journal entries.
  • Device storage: a random app installation identifier used to merge your progress between your own devices.

Why and lawful basis: Account and study data are used to provide the account and study features you request (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Basic service records are also used to keep the app working and secure (Article 6(1)(f), the operator’s legitimate interest in running the tool). Photo attachments and note sharing happen only when you choose those features. Support messages are used to answer you. Cram Deck does not use your notes for advertising, profiling, or automated decisions.

Where information is stored

Your work is saved on your device first. When you sign in and connect to the internet, account data, progress, notes, photos and journal entries sync to the Cram Deck Appwrite Cloud project in Frankfurt, EU. Appwrite hosts the account, database and photo storage services in the selected region. Read about Appwrite regions.

Study progress may also sync to the project owner’s private computers over Tailscale when they are online. These private sync hosts do not make your notes visible to classmates. Apple handles TestFlight distribution and any feedback you choose to send through TestFlight. The public Cram Deck website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub says it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security; see how GitHub Pages works and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Who can see your study content

Cloud records and photo files are permissioned to the account that created them. Other students cannot browse your progress, notes, photos or journal in Cram Deck. Student contributions to shared class material are currently off. If you use the share button, your device sends the selected note or photo to the app or person you choose.

Appwrite provides the cloud service, and the project operator can administer it to maintain the app or respond to a support request. Cram Deck does not sell personal data, show ads, or include third-party analytics in the current app.

How long information is kept

Account data stays in the cloud while your account is active or until you ask for it to be removed. Notes you delete in the app are kept in a recoverable deleted state. To request permanent deletion of your account data, email n00210589@iadt.ie. I’ll remove the account’s cloud records and photo files; copies saved locally on your devices must also be cleared or removed from those devices.

Your choices and rights

You can sign out, remove photos, delete or restore notes, and choose whether to attach a photo or share a note. You can also ask to access, correct, restrict or erase your personal information, or request a portable copy, by emailing the address above. You may raise a concern with Ireland’s Data Protection Commission.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact Fred Bademosi at n00210589@iadt.ie.

This page describes the current app. If Cram Deck’s data practices change, this policy will be updated here.
